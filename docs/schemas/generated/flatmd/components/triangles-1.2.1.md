### triangles (v1.2.1)
Triangles are made up of vertices and indices.

| Property | Type | Description | Flags |
|---|---|---|---|
| vertices | [triangles](../components/triangles-1.2.1-vertices.md) | Vertex coordinates. Columns: x, y, z (the first, second, and third axes of the object's coordinate_reference_system). | ✅ |
| indices | [triangles](../components/triangles-1.2.1-indices.md) | 0-based indices into the vertices. Each triple is a triangle. Columns: n0, n1, n2. | ✅ |
