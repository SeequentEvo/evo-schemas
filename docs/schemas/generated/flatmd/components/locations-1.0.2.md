### locations (v1.0.2)
Locations are coordinates in 3D space. The x, y, z columns follow the axis order of the object's coordinate_reference_system.

| Property | Type | Description | Flags |
|---|---|---|---|
| coordinates | [float-array-3](../elements/float-array-3-1.0.1.md) | Coordinates. Columns: x, y, z (the first, second, and third axes of the object's coordinate_reference_system). | ✅ |
